## 🎓 Educational Context

### Why These Countries Lack Architecture Schools

1. **Economic Factors**
   - Small economies prioritize basic infrastructure over specialized education
   - Architecture education is expensive to establish and maintain

2. **Population Size**
   - Smaller countries (Eswatini: 1.2M, Lesotho: 2.3M) may not need dedicated schools
   - Students often study abroad in South Africa, Nigeria, or Europe

3. **Regional Collaboration**
   - SADC allows student mobility (Eswatini, Lesotho → South Africa)
   - ECOWAS facilitates West African student movement
   - Regional schools like EAMAU in Togo serve multiple countries

4. **Development Stage**
   - Some countries prioritize other sectors (health, agriculture, basic education)
   - Architecture education comes later in development timeline

5. **Conflict/Instability**
   - Somalia: Ongoing security challenges
   - Burundi: Recent political instability
   - Affects both education infrastructure and data availability
