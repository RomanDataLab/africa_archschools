## 📝 Data Collection Challenges

### Information Accessibility
- **Language barriers**: Portuguese (Cape Verde, Guinea-Bissau, São Tomé), Arabic (Mauritania, Somalia)
- **Website availability**: Many universities lack functional websites
- **Data verification**: Difficult to confirm program existence remotely

### Political/Security Factors
- **Conflict zones**: Somalia, South Sudan
- **Disputed territories**: Western Sahara
- **Limited international engagement**: Eritrea

### Economic Factors
- **Small economies**: Limited investment in specialized education
- **Brain drain**: Students often study abroad
- **Regional collaboration**: Some rely on neighboring countries

## 🎓 Educational Development Context

### Why Some Countries Are Missing

1. **Recent Independence**: South Sudan (2011) - still building infrastructure
2. **Small Populations**: Islands like São Tomé, Comoros don't need specialized schools
3. **Regional Collaboration**: Small countries may send students to neighbors
4. **Colonial Legacy**: Some rely on former colonial powers for advanced education
5. **Economic Priorities**: Some prioritize other sectors over specialized architecture education

### Alternative Educational Pathways
- **Online programs**: International distance learning
- **Regional programs**: SADC, ECOWAS, EAC student mobility
- **Scholarship programs**: Study abroad in neighboring countries
- **Technical colleges**: Basic construction/design training without full architecture degree
