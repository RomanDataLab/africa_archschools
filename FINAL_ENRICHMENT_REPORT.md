## Data Quality & Methodology

### Research Sources
- ✅ Official university websites
- ✅ National ministries of education
- ✅ Architecture accreditation bodies (CAA, Archiprix, etc.)
- ✅ Educational databases (Wikipedia, Standyou, Free-Apply)
- ✅ Professional architecture associations
- ✅ Academic research papers and directories

### Verification Process
1. **Institution Existence**: Verified through multiple independent sources
2. **Architecture Programs**: Confirmed presence of architecture/built environment programs
3. **Geographic Coordinates**: City-level centroids provided (approximate)
4. **Website URLs**: Cross-referenced with official domains
5. **Duplicate Removal**: Eliminated duplicate entries by name and URL

### Data Limitations
⚠️ **Coordinates**: City-level approximations, not exact campus locations
⚠️ **Program Status**: Some programs may be suspended or modified
⚠️ **Website URLs**: Some may be outdated or temporarily offline
⚠️ **Accreditation**: Varies by country and institution
⚠️ **Private Institutions**: May have different standards

## Technical Specifications

### CSV Structure
```
Field Name | Type   | Description
-----------|--------|-------------
Institution| String | Full institution name with department
City       | String | Primary city location
Country    | String | Country name (English)
Website    | String | Official university URL
Latitude   | Float  | City-level latitude (decimal degrees)
Longitude  | Float  | City-level longitude (decimal degrees)
```

### Data Format
- **Encoding**: UTF-8
- **Delimiter**: Comma (,)
- **Header**: Yes
- **Quoting**: Automatic for fields with commas
- **Sorting**: By Country (alphabetical), then City (alphabetical)
