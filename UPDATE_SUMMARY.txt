================================================================================
                          ALGORITHM DETAILS
================================================================================

METHOD: Connected Components Clustering
  1. Identify cells with 5,000+ people (high density)
  2. Group connected high-density cells
  3. Calculate total population per cluster
  4. Keep only clusters with 10M+ people
  5. Calculate weighted centroid for each cluster

PARAMETERS (adjustable):
  - min_population: 10,000,000 (cluster threshold)
  - connectivity_threshold: 5,000 (density threshold)

CENTROID CALCULATION:
  - Population-weighted mean position
  - Formula: Σ(position × population) / Σ(population)
  - Represents "center of mass" of population
