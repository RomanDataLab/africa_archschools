# Tanzania Architecture Schools Research

## Date: November 28, 2025

## 🔍 Research Summary

### 🇹🇿 Tanzania - **3 Schools Found**

Tanzania has **THREE universities** offering architecture programs at the university level. There are **no specialized architecture high schools** in Tanzania - students prepare during secondary education by taking subjects like Advanced Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Geography, and Fine Art, which are prerequisites for university architecture programs.

---

## 🎓 Architecture Universities in Tanzania

### 1. ✅ Ardhi University (ARU)
**Already in Dataset**
- **Location:** Dar es Salaam
- **Coordinates:** -6.7924, 39.2083
- **Website:** https://www.aru.ac.tz
- **Programs:**
  - Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch) - 5 years
  - Master of Architecture (M.Arch)
  - Postgraduate Diploma in Architecture (PGD Arch.)
  - PhD in Architecture
- **Notable:** Tanzania's premier architecture institution
- **Admission Requirements:** 2 principal passes in Advanced Math, Physics, Chemistry, Geography, or Fine Art
- **Special Features:**
  - Practical training through fieldwork and industrial training
  - International collaborations with universities worldwide
  - Member of various professional associations

## 📚 Secondary Education Context

### No Architectural High Schools
Tanzania does **not** have specialized architectural high schools. Instead:

**Preparation Path:**
1. **O-Level (Form 1-4):** General secondary education
2. **A-Level (Form 5-6):** Focus on:
   - Advanced Mathematics ⭐
   - Physics ⭐
   - Chemistry
   - Geography
   - Fine Art
3. **University:** Bachelor of Architecture (5 years)

### International Schools
Some international schools in Tanzania offer curricula that prepare students well for architecture:
- **United World College East Africa (UWCEA)** - IB program in Moshi and Arusha
- **Kennedy House International School** - British National Curriculum in Arusha
